About UniShare Uganda

UniShare Uganda is a shared, moderator-reviewed library of notes, slides, summaries and past papers, contributed by students at universities across the country-built so that finding a past paper doesn't depend on which WhatsApp group you're in.

Contact

Questions, issues, or something to report? Reach the UniShare team at hello@unishare.ug.

Terms, in short

By using UniShare, you agree to share only Original or Permitted Academic Material. An account is only needed to upvote, comment, or report - browsing and downloading are free.
